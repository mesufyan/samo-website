Safety

SAMO asks people to meet strangers. That only works if the checks are real and the rules are clear.

What we check before anyone appears in search

A confirmed email address

A code goes to the address and has to come back. Ten minute expiry, five attempts.

A confirmed mobile number

The same again by SMS. A phone number is far harder to churn through than an email address, which is what makes it worth asking for.

A photo of a face

No photo, no listing. Group shots and pictures of scenery do not count.

A profile someone actually wrote

Forty characters minimum, plus what they are here for. Empty profiles are where trouble starts.

Meeting someone the first time

  • Meet in a café, library or park. Never at home, yours or theirs.
  • Tell one person where you are going and when you expect to be back.
  • Make your own way there and your own way back.
  • Keep it short the first time. Half an hour is plenty.
  • If anything feels wrong, leave. You owe nobody an explanation.

Money

Nobody on SAMO should ever ask you for money, for your bank details, for a loan, or for help moving money for someone else. Anyone who does is running a scam, however sympathetic the story. Report them and block them. This is the most common way people who are far from home get hurt.

Reporting and blocking

Every profile has a Report button and a Block button. Blocking is immediate and permanent: they vanish from your results, cannot see you, and cannot reach you from another account tied to the same verified identity. Reports are read by a moderator who speaks the language of the conversation.

We act fastest on: anyone who appears to be under 18, sexual content sent to someone who did not ask for it, threats, and requests for money.

If you are in danger right now

Call your local emergency number. Across the EU that is 112. Report to us afterwards; getting safe comes first.
