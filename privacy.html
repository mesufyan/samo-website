Privacy

Plain version first. This is a template, not legal advice: have a lawyer check it before you launch in the EU.

Template notice. The text below describes how the product is designed to behave. Before you take real signups you need a real privacy policy naming your legal entity, your data protection contact, your lawful basis under GDPR, and your processors.

What we collect

To make an account
Email address, mobile number, password
For your profile
Photo, first name, year of birth, gender, languages, city, bio
If you turn it on
Approximate location, rounded to a 150 m grid
Automatically
Sign-in times and device type, for security

What we never do

  • Sell or rent your data to anyone.
  • Show your exact location to another member.
  • Show your email address, phone number or surname on your profile.
  • Run advertising or third party trackers.

How long we keep it

Your profile lives as long as your account. Location history is deleted after 24 hours. Delete your account and everything else goes within 30 days, apart from records we are legally required to keep, such as a log of a serious safety report.

Your rights

Under GDPR you can ask for a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, or object to how it is used. The first two are self-service in Settings.
